Jesus Rendon-Sifuentes
Audio Engineer | Music Producer

Short Professional Summary

Versatile Audio Engineer, Music Producer, and Live Sound Specialist with a Bachelor of Music in Music Production & Engineering from Berklee College of Music. Founder of Discos Rendon, bringing hands-on expertise across Front-of-House (FOH) and monitor mixing, large-format analog/digital studio recording, Dolby Atmos spatial mixing, podcast post-production, and audio electronics diagnostics.

Roots & Musical Heritage

Rooted in the vibrant cross-border musical traditions of South Texas (Rio Grande Valley) and shaped by intensive studio and live performance environments across Boston, Massachusetts and New York City, Jesus founded Discos Rendon in honor of classic independent record labels and the orange groves of South Texas. His bilingual English/Spanish background and appreciation for warm analog timbre guide every live show, recording session, and custom hardware build.
